# User Personas

> Version 1.0

---

# Who Is RASM Built For?

RASM is designed for industrial engineering organizations involved in the design, review and delivery of complex engineering projects.

The platform serves three categories of users:

• Decision Makers

• Engineering Users

• Project Stakeholders

Each group has different objectives, expectations and success criteria.

---

# Primary Persona

## Engineering Director

### Profile

Responsible for the technical quality of engineering projects.

Oversees multiple engineering disciplines and ensures projects meet quality, budget and schedule objectives.

### Responsibilities

- Engineering governance
- Technical decisions
- Quality assurance
- Team performance
- Standards compliance
- Digital transformation

### Pain Points

- Increasing project complexity
- Engineering resource shortages
- Difficult recruitment
- Late design changes
- Expensive engineering rework
- Loss of engineering knowledge

### Success Metrics

- Higher engineering quality
- Lower project cost
- Reduced engineering hours
- Better project predictability
- Standardized engineering practices

### What RASM Brings

An AI Engineering Copilot capable of continuously reviewing engineering models and reducing technical risks before construction.

---

# Secondary Persona

## CAD / BIM Manager

### Profile

Responsible for CAD and BIM standards, model quality and coordination across design teams.

### Pain Points

- Inconsistent modelling practices
- Manual quality reviews
- Large coordination effort
- Limited review time
- Training new designers

### Success Metrics

- Better model quality
- Standardized reviews
- Faster design validation
- Reduced coordination effort

### What RASM Brings

Continuous model validation with AI-assisted quality control.

---

# Secondary Persona

## Design Engineer

### Profile

Creates engineering concepts and technical solutions.

Works closely with CAD Designers.

### Pain Points

- Manual checking
- Repetitive reviews
- Numerous revisions
- Lack of visibility on downstream impacts

### Success Metrics

- Faster validation
- Better engineering confidence
- Less repetitive work
- Better collaboration

### What RASM Brings

An AI Copilot capable of reviewing engineering decisions and identifying issues early.

---

# Secondary Persona

## CAD Designer / Draftsman

### Profile

Transforms engineering intent into detailed 3D models.

### Industry Context

Experienced CAD Designers are becoming increasingly scarce.

Projects continue to grow in complexity while deadlines become shorter.

### Pain Points

- Repetitive corrections
- Multiple review cycles
- Interpretation of engineering intent
- Time pressure

### Success Metrics

- Fewer corrections
- Better design confidence
- Faster validation
- Better communication with engineers

### What RASM Brings

An intelligent assistant that validates the model during creation rather than after completion.

- Expensive maintenance
- Poor maintainability
- Construction defects
- Long shutdowns

### Success Metrics

- Reliable assets
- Lower lifecycle costs
- Better maintainability
- Higher operational safety

### What RASM Brings

---

# Common Expectations

Regardless of their role, every user expects RASM to:

- Save time
- Improve quality
- Reduce engineering risks
- Reduce project cost
- Improve collaboration
- Preserve engineering knowledge
- Increase confidence in design decisions

---

# User Promise

Every interaction with RASM should reinforce one idea:

"I can make better engineering decisions with greater confidence, in less time and with fewer risks."

---

# Design Implication

RASM must never feel like a traditional CAD software.

It should feel like an intelligent engineering companion that assists every stakeholder according to their role and expertise.
