# RASM Value Proposition

RASM is an AI Engineering Copilot that helps industrial engineering teams deliver projects faster, with higher quality, lower costs and significantly less engineering rework.

Rather than replacing engineers, RASM augments their expertise by continuously reviewing engineering models, detecting risks early and preserving engineering knowledge.

---

# The Promise

Detect engineering issues before they become expensive.

RASM transforms engineering review from a manual, reactive process into an intelligent and continuous workflow.

Instead of discovering problems during construction, fabrication or commissioning, engineering teams identify and resolve them during design.

The earlier an issue is detected, the lower its cost and impact.

---

# Business Value

RASM generates measurable value across the entire engineering lifecycle.

### Reduce Project Costs

By identifying engineering issues earlier, RASM minimizes costly modifications during procurement, construction and commissioning.

Less rework means lower overall project costs.

---

### Improve Engineering Quality

RASM continuously verifies engineering models against:

- Engineering standards
- Company specifications
- Client requirements
- Internal engineering rules
- Best engineering practices

This improves design consistency and reduces human oversight.

---

### Reduce Project Delays

Late engineering corrections often impact procurement, fabrication and construction schedules.

By detecting issues during design, RASM helps engineering teams maintain project timelines.

---

### Reduce Engineering Rework

Engineering rework is one of the largest hidden costs in industrial projects.

RASM identifies clashes, inconsistencies and omissions before they propagate through the project lifecycle.

---

### Increase Engineering Productivity

Highly skilled engineers spend a significant portion of their time performing repetitive design reviews.

RASM automates repetitive verification tasks, allowing engineers to focus on technical decisions, innovation and optimization.

---

### Preserve Engineering Knowledge

Engineering expertise is often undocumented and difficult to transfer.

RASM captures engineering rules, best practices and lessons learned, transforming individual knowledge into organizational knowledge.

---

### Improve Collaboration

Industrial projects involve multiple disciplines working on the same model.

RASM provides a common AI-assisted review process that improves communication between:

- Process Engineers
- Mechanical Engineers
- Piping Engineers
- Structural Engineers
- Electrical Engineers
- CAD Designers
- BIM Managers
- Project Managers
