# 24 — Engineering Experience

## Vision

RASM is not a traditional CAD viewer.

It is an Engineering Intelligence Workspace.

The user should feel like they are operating an advanced engineering mission control system.

---

## Experience Principles

- Engineering first
- Gaming-inspired interactions
- Zero unnecessary complexity
- Fast feedback
- Explainable AI
- Cinematic but professional
- Focus on engineering confidence

---

## Inspiration

- Iron Man / JARVIS
- F1 telemetry
- Minority Report
- High-end simulation software

Not:
- Video games
- Gamification
- Cartoon effects

---

## Motion Language

Every animation has a purpose.

Examples:
- Camera fly-to issue
- Scan effects
- AI analysis progression
- Smooth panel transitions
- Component highlighting

Never use decorative animations.

---

## Analysis Experience

The analysis feels like a mission.

Mission Ready

↓

Initializing Geometry

↓

Recognizing Components

↓

Running Engineering Rules

↓

Generating Engineering Intelligence

↓

Mission Complete

---

## AI Copilot

The assistant behaves like an engineering copilot.

It never chats.

It explains.

It recommends.

It cites engineering rules.

---

## Engineering Feedback

Finding an issue should feel satisfying.

Example:

Issue Detected

Camera focuses automatically

Component highlighted

Recommendation displayed

Engineer can immediately understand the problem.

---

## Visual Identity

Dark workspace

Blue/Cyan lighting

Minimal distractions

Industrial atmosphere

Professional typography

Subtle motion

---

## Design Goal

Young engineers should immediately think:

"This feels like the future of engineering."

Senior engineers should think:

"This looks trustworthy and useful."
