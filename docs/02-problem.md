# The Problem

> Version 1.0

---

# Engineering is changing

Industrial engineering projects are becoming larger, more complex and more multidisciplinary than ever before.

A single project may involve hundreds of engineers, designers and specialists working across multiple disciplines, software platforms and locations.

As projects grow in complexity, engineering reviews become increasingly difficult.

---

# The Hidden Problem

Most engineering errors are not caused by incompetence.

They result from the accumulation of small misunderstandings throughout the design process.

Engineering intent is gradually transformed into engineering models through many human interactions.

Each interaction introduces the possibility of omissions, inconsistencies or interpretation errors.

---

# From Engineering Intent to Reality

A typical industrial project follows this chain:

Engineering idea

↓

Engineering calculations

↓

Technical specifications

↓

CAD / BIM modelling

↓

Engineering reviews

↓

Procurement

↓

Construction

↓

Commissioning

Every transition increases the risk that the original engineering intent is only partially reflected in the final model.

---

# The Growing Shortage of CAD Designers

Experienced CAD Designers and Draftsmen are becoming increasingly difficult to recruit.

At the same time:

• experienced engineers are retiring

• projects become more complex

• deadlines become shorter

• clients expect higher quality

Engineering companies must therefore deliver more with fewer experienced people.

---

# Manual Reviews No Longer Scale

Today, engineering reviews are still largely manual.

Engineers spend hours reviewing:

- piping
- structures
- equipment
- maintenance access
- constructability
- client specifications
- engineering standards

Most of these reviews are repetitive.

Many are performed multiple times during the project.

Despite these efforts, important issues are often discovered too late.

---

# Consequences

Late discoveries generate:

• clashes

• engineering omissions

• constructability problems

• maintenance issues

• safety risks

• engineering inconsistencies

• project delays

• expensive rework

• budget overruns

---

# The Human Cost

Engineers increasingly spend their time checking work that should already be correct.

Instead of designing innovative solutions, they become reviewers.

Their expertise is consumed by repetitive verification tasks.

This creates frustration while reducing engineering productivity.

---

# Why Existing Software Is Not Enough

Current CAD and BIM software primarily help engineers create models.

Some tools detect geometric clashes.

Very few understand engineering intent.

Very few understand engineering rules.

Very few explain WHY something is wrong.

Very few assist engineers in making better decisions.

---

# The Opportunity

Artificial Intelligence can fundamentally transform engineering review.

Instead of manually searching for issues, engineers should collaborate with an AI Engineering Copilot capable of:

- understanding engineering models
- understanding engineering intent
- applying engineering rules
- detecting inconsistencies
- explaining issues
- suggesting corrections
- preserving engineering knowledge

---

# The Future

Engineering teams will not be replaced by AI.

Engineering teams will be augmented by AI.

The organizations that adopt AI-assisted engineering review will deliver projects faster, with higher quality, lower costs and significantly less rework.

That is the future RASM is building.
