# Home Screen

> Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Home Screen is the first contact between the visitor and RASM.

Its mission is not to explain every feature.

Its mission is to create one thought:

**"I need to experience this product."**

The page should feel more like the entrance to a premium engineering platform than a marketing website.

---

# UX Goal

Within 5 seconds the visitor must understand:

• RASM uses AI

• RASM understands engineering

• RASM saves time

• RASM reduces project risk

• RASM is different from traditional CAD software

---

# Emotional Objective

The visitor should feel:

Curiosity

↓

Confidence

↓

Excitement

↓

"I want to try it."
