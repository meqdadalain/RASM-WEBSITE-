# User Journey

> Version 1.0

---

# User Experience Vision

RASM is more than software.

It is an immersive engineering experience that transforms engineering review into an intelligent, collaborative and continuous process.

Every interaction should reinforce one simple idea:

**Engineering review has entered a new era.**

---

# User Goal

The objective is not simply to analyse a 3D model.

The objective is to help engineers make better decisions with greater confidence.

After only a few minutes, every visitor should understand that RASM becomes an indispensable engineering assistant.

# Step 1 — Landing Page

Objective

Create curiosity within a few seconds.

The visitor immediately understands:

• what RASM does

• who it is for

• why it matters

Only one primary action exists.

**Launch Interactive Demo**

---

# Step 2 — AI Initialization

The visitor does not feel like opening a website.

He feels like entering an engineering platform.

Animated sequence:

Initializing AI...

Loading Engineering Knowledge...

Loading Engineering Standards...

Building Digital Twin...

Preparing Analysis Engine...

Ready.

This sequence builds anticipation.

---

# Step 3 — Workspace

The engineering workspace opens.

The interface immediately feels familiar.

Main elements:

• Left Navigation

• 3D Viewer

• AI Assistant

• Dashboard

• Timeline

The interface must be extremely clean.

---

# Step 4 — Engineering Model

The user can either:

Upload a STEP

Upload an IGES

Upload an IFC

Upload another supported CAD model

or

Open a demonstration project.

The process should require only a few seconds.

---

# Step 5 — AI Analysis

This is the emotional peak.

The visitor watches RASM analyse the engineering model.

Example progression:

Reading Geometry...

Understanding Components...

Building Topology...

Checking Engineering Rules...

Checking Standards...

Detecting Clashes...

Checking Accessibility...

Checking Constructability...

Estimating Rework...

Generating Engineering Report...

Every animation reinforces the feeling that the AI truly understands engineering.

---

# Step 6 — Engineering Dashboard

Results progressively appear.

Examples:

Engineering Score

Critical Issues

Warnings

Clashes

Missing Components

Engineering Risks

Maintenance Risks

Constructability Risks

Estimated Rework Cost

Estimated Cost Avoided

Engineering Hours Saved

Project Readiness

The visitor instantly understands the business value.

---

# Step 7 — Issue Explorer

The user selects an engineering issue.

Immediately:

The camera moves to the component.

The component is highlighted.

The violated engineering rule is displayed.

The engineering impact is explained.

Possible corrective actions are suggested.

The objective is understanding.

Not simply displaying an error.

---

# Step 8 — AI Engineering Assistant

The AI becomes the user's engineering companion.

Example questions:

Why is this issue critical?

Which engineering rule is violated?

What is the recommended correction?

How will this affect construction?

Could this impact maintenance?

The AI explains.

It never replaces engineering judgement.

---

# Step 9 — Engineering Report

Generate a professional report.

Contents:

Executive Summary

Engineering KPIs

Detected Issues

Risk Assessment

Engineering Recommendations

Estimated Savings

Project Readiness

The report should be suitable for project reviews and management meetings.

---

# Step 10 — Call To Action

The visitor now understands the value.

The final actions are simple.

Request a Pilot Project

Book a Live Demonstration

Upload Your Own Model
