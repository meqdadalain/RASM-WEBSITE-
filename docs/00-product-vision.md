# RASM Product Vision

> Version 1.0

---

# Vision

RASM is an AI Engineering Copilot that assists engineering teams in reviewing industrial 3D models before construction or manufacturing.

Rather than replacing engineers, RASM augments their expertise by automating repetitive engineering reviews, detecting design issues early and improving collaboration across disciplines.

---

# Mission

Help engineering teams deliver better industrial projects by reducing cost, improving quality, accelerating project delivery and minimizing engineering rework.

---

# The Problem

Industrial engineering projects involve multiple stakeholders:

- Process Engineers
- Mechanical Engineers
- Piping Engineers
- Structural Engineers
- Electrical Engineers
- CAD Designers
- BIM Managers
- Project Managers

Engineering intent is often lost during the transition from concept to detailed 3D models.

As experienced CAD designers become increasingly scarce, design reviews become slower and more prone to errors.

This leads to:

- Design clashes
- Missing components
- Engineering inconsistencies
- Costly rework
- Project delays
- Reduced quality

---

# Our Vision

RASM becomes the AI Engineering Copilot used throughout every industrial project.

It supports engineers by reviewing models continuously, ensuring engineering intent is correctly translated into the digital model while preserving engineering knowledge and reducing repetitive work.
