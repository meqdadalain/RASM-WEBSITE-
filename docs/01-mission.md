# RASM Mission

> Version 1.0

---

# Our Mission

RASM exists to transform the way industrial engineering projects are reviewed.

Our mission is to assist engineering teams with Artificial Intelligence by continuously analyzing engineering 3D models, detecting design issues early and reducing the time spent on repetitive engineering reviews.

RASM does not replace engineers.

It empowers them.

---

# Why RASM Exists

Industrial projects are becoming increasingly complex.

Engineering teams must deliver better projects while facing:

- Shorter project schedules
- Increasing technical complexity
- Pressure on budgets
- Growing quality requirements
- Shortage of experienced CAD designers and engineering reviewers
- Increasing amount of engineering data

Traditional engineering reviews are still largely manual.

As a result, many issues are discovered too late, when they are expensive to correct.

---

# The Human Challenge

Engineering projects rely on collaboration between many disciplines.

An engineer defines the technical intent.

A CAD designer transforms this intent into a detailed 3D model.

Other specialists then review the model for constructability, maintenance, safety, standards compliance and project requirements.

Each handoff introduces opportunities for misunderstanding, omissions or inconsistencies.

These issues are not caused by a lack of competence.

They are a consequence of the growing complexity of modern engineering projects.

---

# Our Belief

Artificial Intelligence should not replace engineering expertise.

Instead, AI should become an Engineering Copilot that:

- performs repetitive checks,
- detects potential risks,
- highlights inconsistencies,
- explains engineering issues,
- proposes possible corrections,
- preserves engineering knowledge,
- supports better engineering decisions.

The engineer always remains responsible for the final decision.

---

# Our Long-Term Mission

Create the world's most trusted AI Engineering Copilot for industrial design.

A platform capable of understanding engineering intent, reviewing industrial 3D models and assisting engineering teams throughout the entire project lifecycle.
